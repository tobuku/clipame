#1

Vyro

MrBeast-backed clipping platform with the highest fixed CPM rate in the market.

Vyro is the platform most clippers want to get on. Backed by MrBeast, it tracks views across TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts and pays a consistent ~$3 CPM. That fixed rate means no guessing about what you'll earn.

The tradeoff is access. Vyro has an application process and doesn't accept everyone. You'll need an established social media presence with decent engagement to get approved. Once you're in, campaigns tend to be high-quality entertainment and lifestyle content from major creators.

  • CPM: ~$3 fixed rate
  • Tracks: TikTok, Reels, Shorts
  • Backed by MrBeast's network
  • Application required
#2

Clipping.net

Open marketplace with the widest variety of campaigns and low barrier to entry.

Clipping.net is where most clippers get their start. It's an open marketplace where creators post campaigns and clippers browse and claim content to edit. CPM rates range from $1 to $5, with SaaS and brand campaigns paying on the higher end.

The platform is beginner-friendly with no strict requirements to join. Campaign variety is the main draw -- you'll find everything from podcasts and gaming to finance and lifestyle. Payouts are bi-weekly, which is faster than most competitors.

  • CPM: $1 - $5 (varies by campaign)
  • Open sign-up, beginner-friendly
  • Wide campaign variety
  • Bi-weekly payouts
#3

ClipAffiliates

Brand matchmaking platform with higher payouts on sponsored campaigns.

ClipAffiliates focuses on connecting clippers with brands and SaaS companies. The platform charges an ~18% total fee and pays clippers $1 to $5 CPM. Brand-sponsored campaigns typically pay at the higher end of that range since advertisers set the budget.

What sets ClipAffiliates apart is the brand matchmaking angle. Instead of just distributing creator content, you're helping brands reach audiences through short-form clips. This means the content tends to be more structured and the briefs more detailed.

  • CPM: $1 - $5 (brand campaigns pay more)
  • ~18% platform fee
  • Brand and SaaS focus
  • Structured campaign briefs
#4

FindClout

US-verified audience network with AI bot detection and 3,000+ faceless pages.

FindClout claims the lowest CPM rates in the market, but makes up for it with volume. The platform has over 3,000 faceless pages and uses AI-powered bot detection to verify that views come from real US-based audiences. Advertisers value this verification, which keeps campaigns flowing.

If you run faceless theme pages on TikTok or Instagram, FindClout is built for you. The platform specializes in distribution through page networks rather than individual creator accounts.

  • US-verified audience tracking
  • AI bot detection
  • 3,000+ faceless pages in network
  • Volume-based earnings model
#5

Promote.fun

Crypto and memecoin-focused platform with fast weekly payouts.

Promote.fun leans heavily into the crypto and Web3 space. CPM rates range from $0.20 to $2.25, with crypto campaigns paying at the top of the range. If you have audiences interested in memecoins, DeFi, or NFTs, this is where those campaigns live.

The lower CPM floor reflects the niche -- crypto campaigns have high volume but lower per-view value compared to brand or SaaS campaigns. Weekly payouts are a plus for clippers who want faster cash flow.

  • CPM: $0.20 - $2.25
  • Crypto/memecoin/Web3 campaigns
  • Weekly payouts
  • High volume, lower per-view rates
#6

ClipFarm

Niche platform for sports betting and prediction market content.

ClipFarm serves the sportsbook and prediction markets niche. If you run sports-focused pages or have audiences engaged in betting content, this platform connects you with campaigns from sports betting brands.

The niche focus means fewer campaigns overall, but the campaigns that do run tend to have solid budgets. Sports betting advertisers spend aggressively on social media distribution.

  • Sportsbook and prediction market campaigns
  • Niche focus with dedicated budgets
  • Sports content clippers
#7

ClipStake

Gambling and casino-focused clipping platform.

ClipStake operates in the gambling and casino niche. The platform connects clippers with online casino, poker, and slots brands looking for short-form video distribution. It's a specialized market with consistent campaign demand.

Like ClipFarm, the niche positioning limits campaign variety but gambling advertisers tend to have larger budgets per campaign. Be aware that gambling content has stricter platform rules on TikTok and Instagram, so compliance matters.

  • Gambling and casino campaigns
  • Higher budgets per campaign
  • Platform compliance important
#8

ContentRewards

Brand campaign distribution platform for general content.

ContentRewards is a straightforward platform for distributing brand content through short-form clips. The platform provides campaign assets and tracking, and clippers earn based on the views their posts generate.

The platform covers a broad range of brand verticals, making it a solid addition to your platform mix. Campaigns are typically pre-packaged, so there's less creative freedom but also less editing work required per clip.

  • Multi-vertical brand campaigns
  • Pre-packaged campaign assets
  • Lower creative effort per clip
#9

Lumina Clippers

Managed clipper network with a hands-off, agency-style model.

Lumina Clippers sits between a platform and an agency. It's a managed network where clippers apply and, once accepted, get assigned campaigns rather than browsing an open marketplace. This means less time hunting for work and more time editing.

The invite-based model means higher quality control and typically better campaigns. If you have a strong portfolio and want steady, assigned work rather than competing for campaigns, Lumina is worth applying to.

  • Managed network model
  • Campaigns assigned to you
  • Higher barrier to entry
  • Steady workflow once accepted

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a clipping platform?

A clipping platform is a marketplace where content creators list their long-form videos (podcasts, streams, vlogs) and short-form video editors ("clippers") claim and edit them into clips for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts. Clippers earn based on views their clips generate, typically measured by CPM (cost per thousand views).

How much do clipping platforms pay per 1,000 views?

CPM rates vary by platform. Vyro pays around $3 per 1,000 views. Clipping.net and ClipAffiliates range from $1 to $5 depending on the campaign. Promote.fun pays $0.20 to $2.25 with higher rates for crypto campaigns. Rates also depend on audience geography, with US and UK views paying more.

Which clipping platform pays the most?

Vyro consistently offers the highest base CPM at around $3 fixed rate. ClipAffiliates can pay up to $5 CPM on brand-sponsored campaigns. The actual earnings depend on your niche, audience location, and clip quality. Most experienced clippers earn $500 to $3,000 per month on a single platform.

Can I use multiple clipping platforms at the same time?

Yes, most clippers use 2-3 platforms simultaneously. Check each platform's terms for exclusivity requirements -- some campaigns require that clips only be posted through their platform. Using multiple platforms lets you diversify income and access different types of campaigns.

How do I get started on a clipping platform?

Sign up on the platform, browse available campaigns, and claim a creator's content to edit. Most platforms require you to have active social media accounts with some following. Start with platforms that have low entry barriers like Clipping.net, then work your way up to invite-only platforms once you have a track record.
